Gym Exercise Finder!

Find exercises for different muscle groups! Follow instructions on how to follow each exercise

This website is designed to help intermediate and even beginner gym goers on learning about different muscle groups! You can find information here based on muscle group, equipment, and category!

Why I made this app

Wether you are beginner, intermediate, or an advanced lifter, the gym has so many different exercises and pieces of equipment that it can sometimes feel overwhelming. I created this to help users quickly find exercises based on the mucle group they want to target. They can learn what specific muscles are targeted, and can learn differnt alternative exercises for when the gym is crowded
